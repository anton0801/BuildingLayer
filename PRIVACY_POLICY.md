# Privacy Policy

## Building Layer

**Effective date:** September 4, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 14, 2026

Building Layer is built to help you plan, document, and evaluate building elements with clarity and confidence. Privacy is part of that approach: your project information belongs to you.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Building Layer (the **“App”**) uses, why it is used, and the choices available to you. Please read it carefully before using the App.

## At a Glance

Building Layer is a local-first application with an optional backup.

- No account, email address, password, or sign-in is required.
- Your work is stored on your device and stays usable with no connection.
- **Cloud Backup is optional and can be switched off at any time in Settings.** When it is on, your project data is copied to our server at buildinglayer.site so it survives losing or replacing your phone.
- Backup is anonymous: the server identifies your device by a random value generated on your phone, not by anything that identifies you.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your information.
- We do not use advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.
- You can delete the server copy from inside the app at any time.

## Information You Create in the App

When you use Building Layer, you may enter or create information to support your work. This can include:

- project and building-element names;
- dimensions, areas, material selections, layer configurations, thermal values, and insulation calculations;
- energy-cost assumptions, units, currencies, and other calculation preferences;
- notes, completion status, reports, and calculation history;
- reminders and their scheduled dates and times; and
- photographs or image markups associated with a building element.

This information is stored locally in the App’s private storage on your device so that the App can display your projects, perform calculations, generate reports, and restore your preferences.

If **Cloud Backup** is switched on, this same information is also copied to our server so it can be restored to a replacement device. If Cloud Backup is switched off, nothing leaves your device.

## Cloud Backup

Cloud Backup is **optional**. It is controlled by a switch in Settings → Cloud Backup and can be turned off at any time, which stops any further data leaving your device.

**How your device is identified.** There is no account. The first time the App contacts the server, your device generates a long random value, keeps it in the iOS Keychain, and uses it to prove that a backup belongs to it. We never receive your name, email address, phone number, Apple ID, advertising identifier, or device serial number, and we have no way to connect a backup to a person.

**What is stored on the server.** Only what you create in the App: element names, dimensions, materials, layer build-ups, calculated values, notes, cost assumptions, reminders, activity history, and — if you attach them — photographs and a signature you draw for a sign-off. Alongside this we store the App version, iOS version, and device model (for example “iPhone”) to help diagnose faults.

**How it is protected.** All traffic uses HTTPS; the server refuses unencrypted connections. Access tokens are stored on the server only as irreversible hashes. Every request is checked against the device it belongs to, so one device cannot read another’s data. Photographs are stored outside the public web directory and can only be retrieved with a valid token.

**How long it is kept.** Your backup is kept until you delete it. Choosing **Settings → Cloud Backup → Remove my data from the server** erases it permanently, including stored photographs. Your projects stay on your device. If you never use the App again, the backup remains until you remove it or ask us to.

**Moving to a new phone.** Because there is no account, the App offers a transfer code: your old phone shows a 9-digit code that is valid once and for ten minutes, and typing it on the new phone moves your projects across. The code is stored on the server only as a hash.

## Your Choices

- **Turn off Cloud Backup** — Settings → Cloud Backup. Nothing further is sent.
- **Delete the server copy** — Settings → Cloud Backup → Remove my data from the server.
- **Delete everything on the device** — Settings → Data → Erase All Data.
- **Remove everything at once** — delete the App after removing the server copy.

## Photos, Camera, and Photo Library

Building Layer may request access to your **camera** or **photo library** only after you choose to attach an image to a building element.

- **Camera access** lets you take a new photo for the selected element.
- **Photo-library access** lets you select an existing image from your device.

Images are used only within the App as part of the project record you create. They are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to Building Layer or any third-party service by the App.

You can decline, limit, or later revoke camera and photo-library permissions in your device settings. If you do, photo-related functionality may no longer be available until permission is restored.

## Notifications and Reminders

If you choose to create a reminder, Building Layer may request permission to send notifications. Notifications are optional.

Approved reminders are scheduled locally through your device’s operating system. Their purpose is limited to notifying you about reminders you created in the App. Building Layer does not use push-notification servers, does not receive notification content, and does not transmit reminder data externally.

You can turn notifications on or off at any time in the App or in your device settings.

## Reports, CSV Files, and Sharing

Building Layer allows you to generate reports and CSV exports from your project data. These files are created locally on your device.

When you tap Share or Export, you choose whether and where to send a file using your device’s sharing interface. Building Layer does not share a report automatically and does not control the information-handling practices of the email, messaging, cloud-storage, or other service that you select. Please review the privacy policy of any service you use to share an export.

## Information We Do Not Collect

Whether or not Cloud Backup is switched on, Building Layer does not collect or receive:

- your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials;
- your precise location or location history;
- contacts, calendar data, microphone recordings, or health information;
- payment-card or other financial-account information;
- advertising identifiers or data used for targeted advertising;
- browsing history or activity in other apps and websites; or
- analytics, diagnostic, or usage data through third-party tracking tools.

The App does not use cookies and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

## How Information Is Used

The information you enter is used to provide the App’s features. All of it is processed on your device; when Cloud Backup is switched on, a copy is also stored on our server so it can be restored later. In particular, it enables Building Layer to:

- save and organize your building elements and projects;
- calculate thermal and energy-related results based on values you provide;
- display attached photos and markups;
- create local reminders;
- generate reports and CSV files when requested; and
- remember your settings and display preferences.

Building Layer does not use your information for advertising, profiling, or automated decisions about you.

## Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you choose to delete it in the App, remove the App, or clear its data through your device settings. If Cloud Backup is switched on, a copy is also kept on our server until you remove it.

You are in control of the content you create. You can remove individual projects, elements, photos, reminders, and other App content through the App where that functionality is available. Removing the App may delete its locally stored data; however, device backups or operating-system features may retain copies according to your device settings and Apple’s policies.

If Cloud Backup has never been switched on, we hold nothing and cannot retrieve, restore, or delete anything for you.

If it is switched on, you can delete the server copy yourself at any time with **Settings → Cloud Backup → Remove my data from the server**. This is immediate and permanent, and it includes any photographs you uploaded. Because backups are anonymous, this in-app control is the only way to identify and remove your data — we cannot locate it for you from an email request, as we have nothing to match you against.

## Data Security

Building Layer keeps your information on your device, and transmits it only when you have switched Cloud Backup on.

When it is on: all traffic uses HTTPS and the server refuses unencrypted connections; the credential that identifies your device is held in the iOS Keychain; access tokens are stored on the server only as irreversible hashes; every request is checked against the device that owns the data, so one device cannot read another’s; and photographs are stored outside the public web directory and served only against a valid token.

The security of locally stored information also depends on your device, operating-system version, passcode settings, and backup configuration. We encourage you to keep your device software up to date and use the security features available on your device.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services, and contains no third-party SDKs. There is no payment processor, authentication provider, or artificial-intelligence service involved in its features.

Cloud Backup is served by our own software running on hosting we rent; the hosting provider stores the data on our behalf and does not use it for any purpose of its own.

If you voluntarily share an exported file using a third-party application or service, that third party is responsible for its own privacy practices.

## Children’s Privacy

Building Layer is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require an account and does not ask for a name, email address, or any other identifying detail, so we do not knowingly receive personal information from its users.

## International Use

Your App data is stored locally on your device. If Cloud Backup is switched on, a copy is also stored on our server, which may be located outside your country of residence; by using Cloud Backup you consent to that transfer. Switching Cloud Backup off keeps your data on your device only.

The App’s features, content, and behaviour are identical in every region.

If you export or share content through a third-party service, that service’s terms and privacy policy will govern any international transfer it performs.

We may update this Privacy Policy if Building Layer’s features or data practices change. If we do, we will revise the **Last updated** date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.
