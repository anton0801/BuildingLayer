Getting started

How do I get a U-value out of it?

Create an element in the Elements tab, then give it a build-up. Two ways:

  • From a template — tap Start from a template and pick an assembly close to yours (insulated cavity wall, warm flat roof, suspended timber floor, and four more). Adjust the thicknesses afterwards.
  • Layer by layer — in the Layers tab, add materials from outside inwards.

The U-Value tab then shows total R, the U-value, and how each layer contributes.

In a hurry: on the last onboarding screen choose Use Sample and you get a fully worked wall to poke at.

Which way round do the layers go?

Outside first, inside last. The cross-section drawing is labelled OUT on the left and IN on the right, and the flame arrow shows heat leaving the warm side.

Order matters for the condensation check, not for the U-value — but getting it backwards will make that check wrong.

Can I work in feet and inches?

Yes. Settings → Units & Currency → Imperial. Every screen switches, including layer thicknesses and the exported report.

Values are stored in SI and converted only for display, so switching back and forth never alters your data.

Cloud Backup

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no email address and no password. Your device identifies itself to the server with a random value it generates on first launch and keeps in the iOS Keychain.

The trade-off is that your backup is tied to that install rather than to a person — which is why the transfer code below exists.

I'm getting a new phone. How do I move my projects?

On the old phone: Settings → Cloud Backup → Get code. A nine-digit code appears.

On the new phone: Settings → Cloud Backup → I have a code and type it in.

The code works once and expires after ten minutes. Anything already in the app on the new phone is replaced by what is being transferred, so export it first if you need it.

After the transfer the old phone stops syncing and keeps its projects as a local copy.

Does the app work with no signal?

Yes — that is the normal case on site. Everything you enter is saved on the device immediately and calculated there. Backup runs in the background when a connection is available.

If Settings shows Offline, your work is safe; it uploads when you reconnect.

How do I delete what's on the server?

Settings → Cloud Backup → Remove my data from the server. It is immediate and permanent, and it includes any photos you uploaded. Your projects stay on the phone.

To stop anything further being sent without deleting what is there, switch Back up my projects off.

Because backups are anonymous, this in-app control is the only way to remove your data — we have nothing to match an email request against.

It says this device was signed out

That happens when the account moved to another phone with a transfer code. Whatever is on this device is untouched and still usable; it is simply no longer backing up.

To back this device up as a separate project set, switch Back up my projects off and on again.

Calculations

Why does my U-value differ from my software at work?

Usually one of three things:

  • Surface resistances. The app adds them automatically by element type (Rsi 0.13 for a wall, 0.10 for a roof, 0.17 for a floor, Rse 0.04). Do not add an "air film" layer yourself.
  • Conductivity. The built-in figures are typical published values. A manufacturer's declared λ for the exact product is usually better — add it as a custom material.
  • Repeating thermal bridges. Timber studs and joists are not averaged in. The Thermal Bridge screen gives an indicative uplift instead.
The condensation check says there's a risk — what now?

The check follows vapour pressure through the build-up using each layer's µ value and flags the point where it reaches saturation. The usual fixes, in order of how well they work:

  • Fit a vapour control layer on the warm side (toggle it on the Condensation screen to see the effect).
  • Move more insulation to the outside of the cold interface, so that point stays warmer.
  • Add a ventilated cavity outboard of the insulation.

The per-interface list shows the local humidity at each point; 100% is where moisture drops out. The outdoor face is greyed because it sits in outside air and is not judged.

This is the simplified steady-state method — appropriate for a site tool and far better than a rule of thumb, but not a transient hygrothermal simulation. Treat it as a warning flag, not a certificate.

Where do the kWh and cost figures come from?

From the heating hours and energy price in Settings → Calculation Defaults. The default is 4380 hours, roughly half a year. Both are yours to change, and every energy and payback figure in the app follows them.

Can I use this for building control or an EPC?

No. Building Layer is an estimating and design tool. It does not certify compliance, produce a legal document, or replace an accredited assessment. Check anything that matters against your regional standard and, where required, an accredited assessor.

Reminders, reports and photos

My reminders aren't arriving
  • Check iOS Settings → Building Layer → Notifications is allowed.
  • Check Settings → Reminders in the app is on, and try Send Test Notification.
  • A reminder must be set for a time in the future — the app refuses a past time rather than silently accepting it.
How do I get a report out?

Reports → Reminders & Reports, choose the sections you want, then Export PDF or Export CSV. Both go through the standard iOS share sheet, so you can mail, save to Files, or send to any app.

The CSV has one row per element and one per layer, grouped, and opens cleanly in Excel and Numbers.

What happens to photos I attach?

They are resized on the device before being stored, kept with the element, and included in the report as evidence. With Cloud Backup on they are also copied to the server, held outside any public directory, and retrievable only by your device.

Deleting the server copy deletes the photos with it.

Data and privacy

What exactly leaves my device?

With Cloud Backup off: nothing. With it on: your elements, layers, materials, notes, reminders, activity history and attached photos, plus the app version, iOS version and device model for diagnostics.

Never: your name, email, phone number, Apple ID, advertising identifier or location. There is no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK in the app at all.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

How do I wipe everything on the device?

Settings → Data → Erase All Data. That clears elements, reminders and history, and resets the material library. It does not touch the server copy — remove that separately if you want it gone.

Requirements

Building Layer runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 14.0 or later. It is free, has no in-app purchases, no subscription and no advertising.
